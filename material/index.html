Paper writing

Tips for Writing Technical Papers

Jennifer Widom · Stanford InfoLab · Writing guide

A practical guide to paper structure, introductions, related work, citations, and presentation. Its introduction framework asks what the problem is, why it matters, why it is difficult, what earlier approaches leave unresolved, and what the new approach contributes.

Useful for planning a section before drafting and checking whether a reader can follow the argument.
