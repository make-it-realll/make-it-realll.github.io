Research

Splitting robot video into its layers

LV Robotics Lab, July to November 2026

In a manipulation video the robot, the object and the background keep hiding one another, most often at the gripper. LV-WM takes a clip and returns the three as separate videos, each complete, with the hidden parts filled in.

I proposed the model, built the agent that labels real robot video without human annotators, and designed the benchmark metrics.

Dexterous manipulation on real robots

LV Robotics Lab, May 2026 to now

I run experiments on a bimanual Franka platform with Sharpa Wave SE and Wuji dexterous hands. I have collected more than 100 demonstrations by teleoperation with GELLO leader arms, Manus gloves and FACTR, and trained π0.5, ACT and flow-matching policies on tasks such as peg-in-hole insertion, handover and pick-and-place.

Course notes

A map of more than 200 university courses in 19 subject lines, from mathematics and computing to philosophy, psychology and history, with self-study notes for each. It covers NUS courses and also the best open courses I could find from other universities. I hope it helps NUS students who are choosing courses see what a course actually covers.

One person cannot write this many pages, so LLM agents draft them to a standard I wrote and keep revising. Pages can still contain errors.

Inspired by csdiy and by Jane Street’s socratic-tutor skill. Course outlines come from NUSMods.
